# 三角函数幂的不定积分

## 1. 正割的幂

对 $\int \sec^n\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta$ 的研究。

**①** $n=1$ 时，$\int \sec\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta$。

注意到

$$
(\sec\theta+\tan\theta)'=\sec\theta(\sec\theta+\tan\theta).
$$

故

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int \sec\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta
&=\int \frac{\sec\theta(\sec\theta+\tan\theta)}{\sec\theta+\tan\theta}\,\mathrm{d}\theta\\
&=\int \frac{\mathrm{d}(\sec\theta+\tan\theta)}{\sec\theta+\tan\theta}\\
&=\ln\lvert\sec\theta+\tan\theta\rvert+C.
\end{aligned}
$$

**②** $n=2$ 时，$\int \sec^2\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta$。显然
$$
\int \sec^2\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta=\tan\theta+C.
$$

**③** $\int \sec^3\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta$。

记

$$
\begin{aligned}
I
&=\int \sec^3\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta
 =\int \sec\theta\,\mathrm{d}(\tan\theta)\\
&=\sec\theta\tan\theta-\int \tan\theta\,\mathrm{d}(\sec\theta)\\
&=\sec\theta\tan\theta-\int \tan^2\theta\sec\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta\\
&=\sec\theta\tan\theta-\int (\sec^2\theta-1)\sec\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta\\
&=\sec\theta\tan\theta-\int \sec^3\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta
  +\int \sec\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta\\
&=\sec\theta\tan\theta-I+\ln\lvert\sec\theta+\tan\theta\rvert+2C.
\end{aligned}
$$

故

$$
I=\frac{1}{2}\sec\theta\tan\theta
 +\frac{1}{2}\ln\lvert\sec\theta+\tan\theta\rvert+C.
$$

**④**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int \sec^4\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta
&=\int \sec^2\theta\,\mathrm{d}(\tan\theta)\\
&=\int (1+\tan^2\theta)\,\mathrm{d}(\tan\theta)\\
&=\tan\theta+\frac{1}{3}\tan^3\theta+C.
\end{aligned}
$$

又观察到，当 $n=3$、$n=4$ 时，可以凑微分求出。

### 递推公式

当 $n\ge 3$ 时，记 $I_n=\int \sec^n\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta$。

则

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_n
&=\int \sec^{n-2}\theta\,\mathrm{d}(\tan\theta)\\
&=\sec^{n-2}\theta\tan\theta
  -\int \tan\theta\,\mathrm{d}(\sec^{n-2}\theta)\\
&=\sec^{n-2}\theta\tan\theta
  -(n-2)\int \tan\theta\sec^{n-3}\theta\cdot(\sec\theta\tan\theta)\,\mathrm{d}\theta\\
&=\sec^{n-2}\theta\tan\theta
  -(n-2)\int \sec^{n-2}\theta\tan^2\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta\\
&=\sec^{n-2}\theta\tan\theta
  -(n-2)\int (\sec^n\theta-\sec^{n-2}\theta)\,\mathrm{d}\theta\\
&=\sec^{n-2}\theta\tan\theta
  -(n-2)\int \sec^n\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta
  +(n-2)\int \sec^{n-2}\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta.
\end{aligned}
$$

即

$$
I_n=\sec^{n-2}\theta\tan\theta-(n-2)I_n+(n-2)I_{n-2}.
$$

即

$$
(n-1)I_n=\sec^{n-2}\theta\tan\theta+(n-2)I_{n-2}.
$$

故

$$
\boxed{
I_n=\frac{1}{n-1}\sec^{n-2}\theta\tan\theta
    +\frac{n-2}{n-1}I_{n-2}\qquad(n\ge 3)
}.
$$

这意味着奇次幂最终降到 $I_1=\int \sec\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta$，偶次幂最终降到 $I_2=\int \sec^2\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta$。

## 2. 余割的幂

再看 $\int \csc^n\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta$。这与 $\int \sec^n\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta$ 大同小异。

**①** $n=1$ 时，$\int \csc\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta$。

注意到

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\csc\theta+\cot\theta)'
&=-\csc\theta\cot\theta-\csc^2\theta\\
&=-\csc\theta(\csc\theta+\cot\theta).
\end{aligned}
$$

则

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int \csc\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta
&=\int \frac{-\csc\theta(\csc\theta+\cot\theta)}{-(\csc\theta+\cot\theta)}\,\mathrm{d}\theta\\
&=\int \frac{\mathrm{d}(\csc\theta+\cot\theta)}{-(\csc\theta+\cot\theta)}\\
&=-\ln\lvert\csc\theta+\cot\theta\rvert+C.
\end{aligned}
$$

**②** $n=2$ 时，

$$
\int \csc^2\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta=-\cot\theta+C.
$$

**③** $n\ge 3$ 时，记 $I_n=\int \csc^n\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta$。

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_n
&=-\int \csc^{n-2}\theta\,\mathrm{d}(\cot\theta)\\
&=-\csc^{n-2}\theta\cot\theta+\int \cot\theta\,\mathrm{d}(\csc^{n-2}\theta)\\
&=-\csc^{n-2}\theta\cot\theta
  -(n-2)\int \cot\theta\csc^{n-3}\theta\csc\theta\cot\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta\\
&=-\csc^{n-2}\theta\cot\theta
  -(n-2)\int \csc^{n-2}\theta\cot^2\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta\\
&=-\csc^{n-2}\theta\cot\theta
  -(n-2)\int \csc^n\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta
  +(n-2)\int \csc^{n-2}\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta.
\end{aligned}
$$

即

$$
I_n=-\csc^{n-2}\theta\cot\theta-(n-2)I_n+(n-2)I_{n-2}.
$$

故

$$
(n-1)I_n=-\csc^{n-2}\theta\cot\theta+(n-2)I_{n-2}.
$$

故

$$
\boxed{
I_n=-\frac{\csc^{n-2}\theta\cot\theta}{n-1}
    +\frac{n-2}{n-1}I_{n-2}\qquad(n\ge 3)
}.
$$

## 3. 正弦与余弦的幂

如果是 $I_n=\int \sin^n\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta$ 呢？

**①**

$$
I_1=\int \sin\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta=-\cos\theta+C.
$$

**②**

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_2
&=\int \sin^2\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta
 =-\int \sin\theta\,\mathrm{d}(\cos\theta)\\
&=-\sin\theta\cos\theta+\int \cos\theta\,\mathrm{d}(\sin\theta)\\
&=-\sin\theta\cos\theta+\int (1-\sin^2\theta)\,\mathrm{d}\theta\\
&=-\sin\theta\cos\theta+\theta-I_2.
\end{aligned}
$$

故

$$
I_2=-\frac{1}{2}\sin\theta\cos\theta+\frac{1}{2}\theta+C.
$$

**③** $n\ge 3$ 时，

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_n
&=-\int \sin^{n-1}\theta\,\mathrm{d}(\cos\theta)\\
&=-\sin^{n-1}\theta\cos\theta+\int \cos\theta\,\mathrm{d}(\sin^{n-1}\theta)\\
&=-\sin^{n-1}\theta\cos\theta
  +(n-1)\int \cos\theta\sin^{n-2}\theta\cos\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta\\
&=-\sin^{n-1}\theta\cos\theta
  +(n-1)\int (\sin^{n-2}\theta-\sin^n\theta)\,\mathrm{d}\theta\\
&=-\sin^{n-1}\theta\cos\theta+(n-1)I_{n-2}-(n-1)I_n.
\end{aligned}
$$

则

$$
\boxed{
I_n=-\frac{\sin^{n-1}\theta\cos\theta}{n}
    +\frac{n-1}{n}I_{n-2}\qquad(n\ge 3)
}.
$$

对于 $I_n=\int \cos^n\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta$，与 $I_n=\int \sin^n\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta$ 大同小异。不再讨论。

## 4. 正切与余切的幂

对于 $I_n=\int \tan^n\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta$。

**①**

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_1
&=\int \tan\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta
 \overset{t=\tan\theta}{=}\int t\,\mathrm{d}(\arctan t)
 =\int \frac{t}{1+t^2}\,\mathrm{d}t\\
&=\frac{1}{2}\int \frac{2t}{1+t^2}\,\mathrm{d}t
 =\frac{1}{2}\int \frac{1}{1+t^2}\,\mathrm{d}(t^2)\\
&=\frac{1}{2}\ln(1+t^2)+C\\
&=\frac{1}{2}\ln(1+\tan^2\theta)+C\\
&=\ln\lvert\sec\theta\rvert+C.
\end{aligned}
$$

（也可直接注意到 $\bigl(-\ln\lvert\cos x\rvert\bigr)'=\tan x$。）

**②**
$$
\begin{aligned}
I_2
&=\int \tan^2\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta\\
&=\int (\sec^2\theta-1)\,\mathrm{d}\theta\\
&=\tan\theta-\theta+C.
\end{aligned}
$$

**③** $n\ge 3$ 时，

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_n
&=\int \tan^n\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta\\
&=\int \tan^{n-2}\theta\tan^2\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta\\
&=\int \tan^{n-2}\theta(\sec^2\theta-1)\,\mathrm{d}\theta\\
&=\int \tan^{n-2}\theta\sec^2\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta-I_{n-2}\\
&=\int \tan^{n-2}\theta\,\mathrm{d}(\tan\theta)-I_{n-2}\\
&=\frac{\tan^{n-1}\theta}{n-1}-I_{n-2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

即

$$
\boxed{
I_n=\frac{\tan^{n-1}\theta}{n-1}-I_{n-2}\qquad(n\ge 3)
}.
$$

对于 $I_n=\int \cot^n\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta$，大同小异。
